// About

Hi, I'm Ian! I'm an AI/ML engineer in New York City. Right now I'm building an LLM-driven personal trainer for Peloton, which is exactly the kind of thing I'd want as an ultra-runner myself.

Most of my career has been spent on recommendation and ranking. At Meta, I worked on recommending small businesses to people, and my models there raised conversion from 8.8% to 9.6%. From there I went to Oracle, where I built a multimodal content moderation system for short-form video and filed two patents on privacy in recommender systems.

I first got into machine learning as an undergrad at Duke, where I spent two years in the Nightingale Lab teaching models to segment prostate anatomy in ultrasound and MRI scans. That's where my interest in ML for biology and health started, and it's stuck with me ever since.
