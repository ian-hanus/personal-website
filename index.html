// About

Hi, I'm Ian! I'm an AI/ML engineer in San Francisco. I build machine learning systems that serve millions of people, and lately, LLM agents.

Right now I'm building an LLM-driven personal trainer for Peloton. As an ultra-runner, this one is personal.

Before that, I worked on large-scale recommendation and ranking. At Meta, I brought multitask learning and graph neural networks to business recommendations, lifting small-business conversion from 8.8% to 9.6% and messaging ads revenue by 3.2%. At Oracle, I built a multimodal content moderation system for short-form video and filed two patents on privacy in recommender systems.

I got my start in ML at Duke's Nightingale Lab, automating prostate segmentation from ultrasound and MRI. I'm still drawn to where ML meets biology and health.
