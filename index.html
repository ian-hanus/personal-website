// About

Hi, I'm Ian! I'm a machine learning engineer in San Francisco. I've spent my career on large-scale recommendation and ranking at Meta, Oracle and Peloton, from graph-based candidate generation to multimodal models for short-form video.

Before industry, I studied electrical engineering and computer science at Duke, where I worked on automated prostate segmentation from ultrasound and MRI. I later did an M.S. in Machine Learning at Georgia Tech.

// Focus

  • Recommender systems & ranking Modeling what people want to see next, at a scale where small gains in relevance reach millions of people. key work: multitask user-behavior models and graph neural network candidate sources for business recommendations at Meta
  • Multimodal content understanding Combining text, OCR and vision signals so models understand what a video is actually about, not just its metadata. key work: end-to-end multimodal content moderation for short-form video at Oracle
  • Privacy & trust in ML Making it possible to verify what a model was trained on, and to measure what it leaks about its training data. key work: 2 patents filed on proof-of-training data and membership inference attacks
